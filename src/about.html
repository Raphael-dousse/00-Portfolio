WHAT ABOUT ME ?

My name is Raphaël Dousse, I am a 20 year old Videographer and Photo Editor. I'm passionate about video and photography, but I also have experiences in sound design and motion design.

I do a lot of post-production and I work on projects such as music videos, cinematic videos, street photography and various other projects. I'm also on social media as toyakuedt, where I share my work and my projects.

At first, I was just doing amateur editing of Japanese anime series and movies, which is where my Instagram name comes from.. It was really trendy on Instagram around 2020 and 2023. But ever since I was little, I’ve really loved watching movies, and that’s what sparked my fascination with imagery. It’s about creating and expressing a vision, a feeling that I want to share and say, “Hey! Look, this is what I see!” So that's where it all came together. A key moment, very tight cuts, music that flows seamlessly, and bouncy transitions.

It's sort of where it all started, because that's what inspired me to take it further and launched my career in audiovisual. So it's important for me to be under that name and keep these origins from my story.

So yeah ! I like music and visual, and mixing them gives something really funny to do.
